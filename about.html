About

I'm an MS student in the Environmental, Resource, and Energy Policy program at Colorado School of Mines, focused on modernizing the electric grid.

Before this, I spent about a year in early-stage investing after finishing my MBA at CU Boulder. I liked the work, but I wanted to be part of the actual change, not just watching from the outside. That's what brought me to energy policy, and specifically to the Colorado Public Utilities Commission, which I think is one of the most impactful places to start.

This site is where I track what I'm learning: PUC activity, books, podcasts, and my own notes, as I work toward a career in the energy sector.
